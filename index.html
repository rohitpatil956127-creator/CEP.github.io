Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about career planning and our services

How accurate is the career assessment quiz?
Our career assessment is based on established career theory and has been validated with thousands of users. While no assessment can be 100% accurate, our quiz has shown a 85% satisfaction rate among users who followed our recommendations.
Is this service completely free?
Yes, our basic career exploration tools, assessment quiz, and resource library are completely free. We offer premium services like one-on-one mentorship and detailed career planning for a fee, but the core features remain free.
How often is the career information updated?
We update our career data quarterly to reflect the latest job market trends, salary information, and educational requirements. Our team continuously monitors industry changes to ensure you have the most current information.
Can I use this if I'm considering a career change?
Absolutely! Our platform is designed for students exploring their first career as well as professionals considering a career change. We provide specific guidance for career changers, including transferable skills assessment and transition strategies.
